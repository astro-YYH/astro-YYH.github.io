Welcome to Yanhui's homepage!

I am currently a Ph.D. student studying theoretical and computational astrophysics and cosmology at the University of California, Riverside. Learn more about me by exploring the website, and feel free to contact me if anything interests you!

My research focuses on cosmology and galaxy formation, using supercomputers and advanced numerical techniques to model the Universe's complex evolution. My work spans a wide range of spatial scales—from turbulent mixing layers in the medium surrounding galaxies (~pc) to individual galaxies (~kpc) and the large-scale structure of the Universe (~Mpc). These theoretical predictions are essential for interpreting data from major astronomical surveys, including the Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope, LSST, CSST, and JWST. By comparing predictions from simulations with observations, we can identify the models that best describe galaxy formation and evolution and the growth of cosmic structures from primordial perturbations.
